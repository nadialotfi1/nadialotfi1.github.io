About Me

A little context on how I think, what I’m learning, and what I’m working toward.

Who I am

I'm a Northwestern University student studying Biomedical Engineering because I am drawn to issues that connect science, medicine, and design to improve care. I enjoy the process of using creativity and problem solving to move from a question or idea to something tangible. The common thread in my experiences and projects has been learning to develop human-centered, safe designs grounded in real problems.

What I care about

I care about building things that are thoughtful, useful, and grounded in real needs. I’m interested in research and projects that combine technical depth with a clear understanding of the people they are meant to serve.

I also care about access to opportunity, education, and creating work that has a meaningful impact beyond the classroom.

More broadly, I value responsible innovation, learning hard things, curiosity, and working with people who challenge me to think better.

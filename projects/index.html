Biomedical Instrumentation

Oscillometric Blood Pressure Monitoring System

2026

Add Main Blood Pressure Image

Problem

Develop a system capable of extracting meaningful physiological information from cuff-pressure signals during non-invasive blood pressure measurement.

Approach

Built an instrumentation chain using pressure sensing, instrumentation amplification, high-pass and low-pass filtering, and Python-based analysis of the resulting oscillometric signal.

Technical Skills

Python Signal Processing Analog Circuits Instrumentation Frequency Analysis Peak Detection

Results

The system successfully isolated arterial oscillations and used the oscillation envelope to estimate systolic, mean arterial, and diastolic pressure.

135.7 Estimated systolic pressure, mmHg
89.3 Estimated mean arterial pressure, mmHg
70.4 Estimated diastolic pressure, mmHg

Mechanical Design

Bicycle Caliper Brake Design

2026

Add Main Brake CAD or Prototype Image

Problem

Design and manufacture a lightweight bicycle caliper brake capable of meeting structural and braking requirements while remaining practical to install and use.

Approach

Used analytical modeling, CAD, finite element analysis, topology optimization, SLS prototyping, and physical testing to iteratively improve stiffness, stress distribution, mass, and braking performance.

Technical Skills

SolidWorks FEA Topology Optimization Mechanical Design Prototyping Design Testing

Results

The final design met the established braking and structural targets while substantially reducing right caliper deformation through iterative redesign.

96 g Final brake system mass
~8 → ~3 mm Right-caliper FEA deflection reduction
13.4 m Rescaled dry braking distance at 25 km/h
